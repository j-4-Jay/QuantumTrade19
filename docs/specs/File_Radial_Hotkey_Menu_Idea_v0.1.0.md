# Radial Hotkey Menu (Space bar) - Idea v0.1.0 (QT19, 07-11-2026)
Status: direction approved by Jay 07-11-2026. Not built. Examples were two pictures made in thread 008 (not saved in the project).

## What Jay asked
- Space bar inside the software opens a circular menu, like the Maya hotbox.
- Menus and submenus, with highlight when the mouse is over an item.
- Universal key, but the content changes with the page.
- Less mouse travel, instant info without a page change, never cluttered.
- Very prominent text sized by level. Premium, royal, costly look.

## Design rules (proposal)
1. Open at the mouse position on Space; close with Space again, Esc or a click outside. Hold-and-release choice is a later option.
2. Ring 1: at most 8 wedges. Ring 2 (submenu): at most 6 items, opens on hover as an outer arc next to the hovered wedge.
3. Centre: page name as heading, plus one live line (symbol, timeframe, status).
4. Same position for the same action on every page (for example Back at the bottom, Settings at the lower right) so the hand learns it.
5. Page menus change by page: Chart, Setups, POIs, Orders and Risk, Journal, Settings.
6. Info without a page change: a small info card beside the hovered item (for example last setup state, RRR, price).
7. Text sizes: heading small caps, ring 1 large bold, ring 2 medium, hints small and muted.
8. Look: midnight navy glass, thin gold lines, ivory text, gold glow and slight scale on hover, emerald and crimson only for Win and SL.
9. Reduced Motion setting must switch the animations off.
10. Space must not fire while typing in a text box.

## Chart page example (draft, to confirm with Jay)
Timeframe, Go to Live, Hide Candles, Callouts, Drawings, Symbols, Replay, Alerts.
