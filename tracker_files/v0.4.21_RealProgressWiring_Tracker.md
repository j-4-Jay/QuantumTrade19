## 1. What was actually broken

Inspecting your live source files proved the deployed app was running an
older, incomplete generation of the download/progress pipeline:

- `market_data_monitor.py` built `DeepHistoryDownloaderWorker` WITHOUT its
  `get_missing_ranges`/`get_coverage` callbacks, so it silently used old
  manifest-based math instead of real SQLite row counts.
- `get_deep_history_progress()` returned only `covered_days`,
  `is_complete`, `state`, `error` - no percent, no present/required/
  missing counts, no ETA at all.
- `trading_panel_mixin.py`'s progress bar computed its own FAKE percent
  as `local_days / requested_days * 100`, completely ignoring the real
  download status the downloader was already tracking internally.

This is exactly why the progress bar barely moved and the ETA was "just
animating" - there was no real signal anywhere in the pipeline for it to
bind to.

## 2. Fix Applied (4 files)

- `candle_store_worker.py`: added `get_local_coverage()` and
  `get_missing_ranges()` - real SQLite-row-backed coverage/gap math.
- `market_data_monitor.py`: wires those two methods into
  `DeepHistoryDownloaderWorker`; `get_deep_history_progress()` now returns
  the FULL real status (percent, present/required/missing candles,
  eta_seconds, broker_ceiling_reached).
- `trading_panel_mixin.py`: `trading_panel_backfill_percent` is now REAL
  (read from the downloader's own status via a new
  `trading_panel_tf_progress` dict, refreshed every poll tick). New
  `trading_panel_eta_text` computed var gives a real human-readable ETA,
  broker-ceiling message, or error - not a fake animation.
- `app_state.py`: added the `trading_panel_tf_progress` field.
